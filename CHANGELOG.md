# Changelog

## 2.3
- **Backup links.** Edit a subscription (Settings → Subscriptions) and add the other links of the same subscription under Backup links, one per line. When the server stops answering, FIRE tries them in order, moves to the first that works and keeps it, so lists, channels and films carry on. A channel or film that keeps failing also looks for a link that answers. Saving a subscription signs in on the first of its links that answers.

## 2.2
- **US Movies & Series and Shia Channels now show.** 2.1 kept whatever it had found the first time for 12 hours, so a package that didn't answer then stayed missing. Now the three ready-made packages are looked for again each time the app opens (or Refresh is pressed) until all three are there, and a channel not found in its usual package is found by name in the full channel list.

## 2.1
What's new in FIRE on the TV (up to its 6.3), on iPhone and iPad.
- **Ready-made Live packages.** After Big Leagues: 🎬 US Movies & Series (HBO, Showtime, Starz, Cinemax, AMC, FX…), 📰 News (Arabic first, then English), and 🕌 Shia Channels. One card per channel, its HD copy when there is one. Each has an All card.
- **Hide packages.** Press and hold a package's All card and choose Hide this package. In the Packages list, tap Select to tick several (or Select All) and hide them together. The eye button next to the search field shows how many are hidden and brings them back (tick some, or Select All, then Unhide). Each tab and each subscription keeps its own.
- **Quality.** On a live channel, the 4K button lists the same channel from the provider's other packages (HD, 4K, RAW, HEVC, SD…), sharpest first, the Arabic packages' copies before other countries'. The title shows the size really playing, since names often promise more. Works for Big Leagues and the ready-made packages too.

## 1.6
- **Picture size.** Double-tap a film or episode, or tap the size button, to step through Original size, Fill screen, and Stretch — handy for phone-shot vertical clips (the AI series) that would otherwise sit small in the middle.
- **Short clips are remembered.** Very short episodes (a minute or two, like the AI series) now stay in Continue Watching and keep their place, where before they were always dropped.
- **Remove several at once.** Long-press a card in Continue Watching or Recent Channels and choose Select Multiple to tick several, use Select All, and delete them together instead of one by one.

## 1.5
Everything new in FIRE on the TV (up to its 3.5), on iPhone and iPad.
- **Big matches on the Live tab.** From a day and a half before kickoff until full time, each match of the big teams (the five big leagues, their cups and the Champions League) gets a row with the channels showing it. The guides are read on Wi-Fi only; on mobile data the last rows found stay.
- **Big Leagues package.** beIN, Sky, DAZN, Movistar, Canal+ and the other channels that carry these leagues, in one row with an All card.
- **LaLiga · Copa del Rey · Champions League row.** The channels showing them in the week ahead.
- **Recent Channels.** The last eight channels watched for 5 seconds or more. Press and hold one to take it off.
- **Previous channel.** In the player, the ↩︎ button goes back to the channel you were on before, even in another package; tap again to come back.
- **Skip Intro.** In a series episode's first minutes. Where you settle after it is remembered, so the next episodes skip right to it.
- **Subtitles and Sound lists.** Subtitles: Off, Arabic, English, then the rest, with the one showing ticked. Sound only shows when there is more than one track. Arabic subtitles first, else English; a film's own default in another language no longer wins. Track names drop codes like "ara".
- **Film and series pages.** Starring and Director lines, a Trailer button (YouTube), and More Like This from the same section.
- **Foreign titles with Arabic subtitles first.** Movies and Series open with the foreign and subtitled packages, then the platforms' own, dubbed, Arabic, and other countries' last. Search ranks copies the same way.
- **Packages.** The button beside the search field lists every row of the tab; search it by name and open one.
- **All card.** The first card of every package row (and of Continue Watching and Big Leagues) opens the whole package as a grid, with its own search field.

## 1.4
- **Next episode.** While watching a series, a Next Episode button sits above the time bar.
- **Plays on by itself.** In the last 15 seconds of an episode a card shows the next one with a countdown; it starts on its own when the episode ends, even across seasons. Tap ✕ to stop at the end instead.
- **Brightness and volume by sliding.** In the player, slide up or down on the left half for brightness and on the right half for volume, like Netflix and Apple TV. The brightness goes back to how it was when you leave the player.

## 1.3
- **Update from inside the app.** Settings shows your version and checks for a new one. Tap Download, then open the file with your signing app to install it.
- **Storage stays small.** Saved channel, film and series lists are kept under 80 MB, oldest first.
- **Refresh clears pictures too.** Refresh all lists in Settings now also clears saved posters.

## 1.2
- **Pages fit the screen.** On iPhone the series and film pages no longer slide off the left edge, and on iPad the Play and Info buttons are no longer cut off under the big picture.

## 1.1
- **Search is ready sooner.** The lists search uses are prepared in the background on Wi-Fi, so the first search no longer waits a minute. Mobile data is never used for this.
- **Clearer player.** While a film loads, the loading wheel takes the play button's place instead of sitting on top of it.
- **Solid top bar.** Rows scrolling under the tabs no longer show through them.

## 1.0
- **FIRE on iPhone and iPad.** Live TV, Movies and Series from your IPTV account, in the same orange-on-dark look as FIRE on the TV.
- **Fits every screen.** Every iPad size and every iPhone, in portrait and landscape.
- **Plays everything.** Films and episodes in MKV, 4K and HEVC, with Arabic subtitles chosen by themselves when the film has them.
- **Continue Watching and My List.** Pick up a film or episode where you left it; keep favourites in each tab.
- **Search inside each tab.** Arabic titles first, sharpest copies first.
- **Live guide.** What's on now and next, and every package beside the picture.
