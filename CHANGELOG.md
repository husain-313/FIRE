# Changelog

## 1.0
- **FIRE on iPhone and iPad.** Live TV, Movies and Series from your IPTV account, in the same orange-on-dark look as FIRE on the TV.
- **Fits every screen.** Every iPad size and every iPhone, in portrait and landscape.
- **Plays everything.** Films and episodes in MKV, 4K and HEVC, with Arabic subtitles chosen by themselves when the film has them.
- **Continue Watching and My List.** Pick up a film or episode where you left it; keep favourites in each tab.
- **Search inside each tab.** Arabic titles first, sharpest copies first.
- **Live guide.** What's on now and next, and every package beside the picture.
